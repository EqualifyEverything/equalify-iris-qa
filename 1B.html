Test 1B: CSS-only inline emphasis

Variation: Inline emphasis marked up with <span class="italic"> and <span class="bold"> plus CSS. No page markers or navigation; only the emphasis markup is under test.

Test: Read the paragraphs continuously with a screen reader. Note whether each emphasized phrase is announced, whether it breaks the sentence into separate chunks, and whether the sentence still reads naturally. Record browser and screen reader.

This is a test of the emergency alert system. The emergency alert system is a useful tool for ensuring that citizens are aware of emergencies.

Residents should follow the guidance in The Public Safety Handbook and keep a battery-powered radio nearby. Instructions marked do not travel apply to everyone in the affected area, even if conditions appear calm.

A warning can be both urgent and specific, and it should be repeated through sirens, text messages, and radio broadcasts until the threat has passed.
